## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a fusion of dermatological efficacy and Korean glass-skin luxury. The aesthetic targets skincare connoisseurs seeking clinical performance wrapped in an editorial, sensory ritual. 

The visual style blends **Editorial Minimalism** with **Luminous Glassmorphism**:
- Soft, translucent surfaces reflecting hydration and radiance.
- Ample negative space mirroring dermatological purity and high-fashion spreads.
- High-contrast editorial typography paired with modern, balanced utility fonts.
- Refined micro-interactions that evoke gentle fluid movement and subtle ambient glows.

## Colors

The palette balances soft, glowing skincare tones with grounded luxury contrasts:
- **Primary (`#AF236D`)**: Deep raspberry wine drawn from clinical K-beauty antioxidants, commanding call-to-actions, key active states, and focal branding.
- **Secondary (`#C48B71`)**: Burnished rose bronze metallic for luxury tags, badges, ratings, and delicate trim lines.
- **Tertiary (`#F0B8C8`)**: Dewy blush pink representing hydration, active serums, and gentle luminous focus states.
- **Neutral (`#241F21`) & Deep Obsidian (`#1A1A1A`)**: Warm charcoal ink delivering editorial legibility without the harsh coldness of raw black.
- **Backgrounds (`#FAF8F5`, `#FCECEF`)**: Alabaster cream and blush mist forming warm, soothing canvas layers that let glassmorphic panels glow.

## Typography

The type system pairs high-fashion editorial display type with crystal-clear utility sans:
- **Display & Headlines (`Bodoni Moda`)**: Evokes luxury editorial packaging, French and Korean couture glossies, and fine typography. Display numbers and titles utilize standard ligatures and slight negative letter-spacing for refined elegance.
- **Interface & Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers clear clinical legibility for ingredient decks, pricing matrixes, clinical trial stats, and navigation.
- **Labels & Microcopy**: All uppercase with generous tracking (`0.08em` to `0.15em`) for category anchors, badges, and brand sub-signatures.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column responsive fluid grid provides rhythmic structure across large monitors down to compact mobile displays:
- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns, 64px (`margin`) outer edge, 24px (`gutter`) gap. Maximum content container width is capped at 1360px for controlled visual cadence.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns, 32px (`margin-tablet`) outer edge, 20px gutter.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px)**: 4 columns, 20px (`margin-mobile`) outer edge, 16px (`gutter-mobile`) gap.
- **Generous Vertical Breathing Room**: Hero sections and curated editorial product collections rely on `space-2xl` and `space-3xl` intervals to replicate physical luxury magazines.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth mirrors the dewy, layered reflection of glass skin:
- **Base Canvas**: Tinted warm alabaster (`#FAF8F5`) or subtle petal gradient.
- **Glassmorphic Surface**: Frosted white panels (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)`) backed by a `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and bounded by a hairline border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)` top/left, `rgba(240, 184, 200, 0.3)` bottom/right).
- **Luminous Shadow**: Low-opacity ambient shadows with soft rose tinting: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(175, 35, 109, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(45, 39, 39, 0.03)`.
- **Active / Floating States**: Elevated hover cards translate 4px along the Y-axis accompanied by a rose-gold micro-glow: `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -8px rgba(196, 139, 113, 0.18)`.

## Shapes

The design uses soft, organic, pebble-like contours inspired by smooth cosmetic ampoules, skincare drops, and the fluid brand emblem:
- **Standard Surfaces & Cards**: `rounded-md` (0.5rem / 8px) to `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) for balanced modern discipline.
- **Interactive Badges, Pills & Floating Triggers**: Fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to echo fluid droplet contours.
- **Hero Accent Containers**: Asymmetrical, organic pill masks mirroring the soft background silhouette seen in the brand identity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Deep wine `#AF236D` background, high-contrast white text, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), smooth 250ms ease hover transitioning into `#8E1B57` with a soft rose-gold ambient halo.
- **Secondary / Luxury Editorial**: Frosted glassmorphism pill, obsidian text, 1px border (`#C48B71` at 40% opacity), lifting 2px on hover with warm champagne fill tint.
- **Text Button / Editorial Link**: Uppercase `label-md` tracking, accompanied by a subtle animated underline that expands from center on hover.

### Product & Editorial Cards
- **Catalog Card**: Off-white alabaster backdrop with seamless inner padding, image container framed with subtle rounded-lg corners, pill badge top-left denoting dermatological certification or formulation type.
- **Quick-Add Microinteraction**: Floating circular frosted-glass button with a rose-gold icon appearing smoothly upon card hover.
- **Pricing & Tags**: Currency and price displayed in bold `Plus Jakarta Sans` next to high-fashion serif title.

### Chips & Formulation Badges
- **Glass Pills**: Translucent gradient fill (`#FCECEF` to `#FAF8F5`), 1px translucent border, uppercase `label-sm` tracking with icons indicating "Clinically Tested", "Glass Finish", or "Clean K-Beauty".

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields**: Minimalist warm white containers, understated borders (`rgba(45, 39, 39, 0.12)`), transitioning on focus to an illuminated rose-bronze boundary ring with a delicate 2px blush aura.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Custom circular rings with smooth fill transitions using `#AF236D` and gold-accented checkmarks.

### Special E-Commerce Modules
- **Ingredient Spotlight Card**: Frosted glass drawer displaying hero botanical compounds with circular visual swatches and benefit meters.
- **Routine Step Indicator**: Numbered champagne-bronze circles connected by delicate hairline dividers showing AM/PM skincare progression.
